What is Autonomous Operations?

Autonomous operations is the convergence of AI, observability, and reliability engineering. It's not just about chatbots answering questions — it's about AI agents that can diagnose incidents, execute remediation runbooks, navigate ops consoles, and coordinate across toolchains with appropriate safety guardrails.

This list curates the essential building blocks for AI-powered SRE, DevOps, and SecOps workflows: graph-based root cause analysis over logs and metrics, agentic systems that troubleshoot and heal production, Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers that safely expose operational tools to AI, and browser automation frameworks that let agents interact with dashboards and consoles.

Project Categories

🔍 Graph RAG & Root Cause Analysis

Tools that perform retrieval-augmented generation, graph-based reasoning, and correlation analysis over operational telemetry to identify incident root causes.

🤖 Agentic Remediation & Runbooks

Systems where AI agents autonomously execute or orchestrate remediation actions, runbooks, and operational procedures.

🔌 MCP Servers & Gateways

Model Context Protocol servers and gateways that expose operational tools with appropriate security controls.

🖥️ Browser & Desktop Ops Agents

Browser automation frameworks and desktop agents that let AI navigate operational consoles and dashboards.

🛡️ Compliance & Safety

Tools and frameworks for establishing guardrails, approval workflows, and audit trails.

🧪 Datasets & Simulators

Datasets, simulation environments, and hands-on labs for developing and testing autonomous operations systems.
